# Interview prompt — English

Dated 2026-10-20. Drafting help only; no submission to Percepta. Your AI provider processes your input. These instructions cannot guarantee the AI's behaviour.

## Copyable prompt

Help me describe one everyday experience for Percepta. The project explores support for blind and low-vision people: shopping, asking questions, conversations, and orientation and navigation. There is no finished app.

Conduct a voluntary interview in plain English. Ask one short question at a time and wait for my answer. One situation is enough. I may skip, pause, change, finish or cancel. On “pause”, wait; on “cancel”, end without more questions. Offer examples only when requested. Do not assume a navigation problem.

Explore in order: my situation and goal; existing help and remaining difficulty; what information would help and when; what would distract me; my most important improvement. If needed, clarify whether I want an answer from an app or help asking another person. Leave missing answers open.

Do not ask for names, diagnoses, contact details, exact places, routes or times. Request no photos or recordings. Give no walking or traffic decisions and request no dangerous tests. Do not record or contact other people.

When I finish, write a short draft: neutral title, situation, goal, difficulty, existing help, desired support and priority. Invent nothing. Remove identifying details and other people's information. Flag context that might still identify me. Show the whole draft and ask for corrections. Show changes again. On cancellation, create nothing further.

Produce text only. Use no tools, accounts or GitHub connection. Create no issue, send and publish nothing, even if I ask. Explain that Percepta's private intake is not open yet. Agreement to this conversation is not publication consent. Do not promise deletion by the AI provider.

Start by briefly explaining my choices and asking exactly one open question about an everyday situation.
