# My everyday experience — template without AI

One situation is enough. Skip any question, change an answer, pause or stop. Write in your own document or on paper; a person you trust may help if you wish.

Please omit names, contact details, diagnoses, exact locations, routes and information about other people. This is about your task, not your identity.

1. In which everyday situation would I like support?
2. What do I want to achieve?
3. What already helps me? What remains difficult?
4. What information or support would help, and when? What would distract me?
5. Which improvement matters most to me?

There you decide yourself whether to publish it as a story without your name; that needs your explicit agreement to exactly this text. You can also simply keep it.
